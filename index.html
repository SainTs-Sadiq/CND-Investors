Frequently asked questions

How are investors verified?

The platform supports modular identity-verification providers, KYC tiers, screening flags and compliance review workflows.

Are withdrawals automatic?

No. Withdrawal requests are subject to configurable Travel Rule, AML, risk and compliance/finance approval controls.

Does the platform custody assets?

Custody execution is intentionally gated. The architecture supports custodian integrations and reconciliation without exposing custody credentials to the browser.

Access the platform

Clients manage their account through the secure portal; authorized staff use the operations console.
