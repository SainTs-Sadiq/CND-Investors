Is CND Investors real?

No. The current demo uses browser storage and has no payment, custody or blockchain integration.

What would production require?

A secure backend, authentication, KYC/AML, transaction controls, custody/payment integrations, monitoring, audit trails and applicable legal/regulatory review.
